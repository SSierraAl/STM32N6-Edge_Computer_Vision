Understanding the "Memory Map"
Think of your external flash like a bookshelf. You have three books on it:

Book 1 (FSBL): 0x70000000 to 0x700FFFFF

Book 2 (Your App): 0x70100000 to 0x7037FFFF

Book 3 (AI Model): 0x70380000 to the end

If you "Full Chip Erase," you clear the whole shelf. If you use "Skip Flash Erase" (or Sector Erase), you just take out Book 2 and put the new version in.
